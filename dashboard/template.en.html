How it was built

The approach I use with real plant data.

Physics firstPower proportional to irradiance, corrected for module temperature (−0.4%/°C). A single fitted parameter.
ML on the residualLightGBM learns what physics doesn't explain: shading, inverter clipping, soiling, time of day.
Honest validationThe last 10 days are kept out of training. An extra test with only 5 days of history.
Inverter healthEach inverter against its neighbours at the same instant: separates equipment faults from weather effects.

The model uses measured irradiance, so it estimates expected production. Fed with a weather forecast it can forecast production, with a larger error that depends on the quality of that forecast.
